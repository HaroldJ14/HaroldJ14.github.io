Sonic The Hedgehog

Sonic the Hedgehog is a caring blue hedgehog, introduced to the world in 1991's hit game, Sonic the hedgehog. He has wonderful friends and uses his abilities to defeat Robotnik, also known as Eggman. He is Sega's main mascot. His rival is Shadow, who looks similarly to him. Today, Sonic has many games centered around him. There have been some mishaps, like Sonic the Hedgehog (2006), which had inconsistent storylines and a ton of bugs. He is extremely fast and has green eyes.
