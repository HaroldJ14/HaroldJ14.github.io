Team Sonic:

Sonic The Hedgehog

Sonic the Hedgehog is a caring blue hedgehog, introduced to the world in 1991's hit game, Sonic the Hedgehog. He has wonderful friends and uses his abilities to defeat Robotnik, also known as Eggman. He is Sega's main mascot. His rival is Shadow, who looks similar to him. Today, Sonic has many games centered around him. There have been some mishaps, like Sonic the Hedgehog (2006), which had inconsistent storylines and a ton of bugs. He is extremely fast and has green eyes. He likes:

  • Chilli dogs,
  • Saving the world,
  • Saving animals,
  • And spending time with his friends.

Tails the Fox

Tails the fox AKA Miles Prower is a brainy young fox. He has orange fur, blue eyes, and enjoys spending time with his very best friend, Sonic the Hedgehog. He also enjoys:

  • Eating mints,
  • Making inventions,
  • And flying his plane, "The Tornado".
